Confidence Intervals

BUSINESS STATISTICS · J. ALEJANDRO GELVES
Quick Reference
01 · The Shape

Confidence Interval

estimate ± margin of error
The margin of error is a critical value times the standard error. 95% confidence means α = 0.05.
EXAMPLE
90% CI: 78.1 ± 1.05 = [77.05, 79.15]
02 · Mean, σ Known

Z-Interval

x̄ ± zα/2 · σ / √n
Use the normal critical value when the population standard deviation σ is known.
EXCEL
=CONFIDENCE.NORM(α, σ, n)
03 · Mean, σ Unknown

t-Interval

x̄ ± tα/2 · s / √n
When σ is estimated by s, use the t-distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom. Its heavier tails give a wider interval.
EXCEL
=CONFIDENCE.T(α, s, n)
04 · Proportion

Proportion Interval

p̂ ± zα/2 · √(p̂(1 − p̂) / n)
The sample proportion is approximately normal, so a z critical value is used.
EXCEL
=NORM.S.INV(1-α/2)*SQRT(p̂*(1-p̂)/n)
05 · Critical Values

z and t

z: 1.645 (90%) · 1.96 (95%)
The multiplier that sets the confidence level. The t value is larger for small samples and close to z for large ones.
EXCEL
=NORM.S.INV(1-α/2) · =T.INV.2T(α, df)
06 · Planning

Required Sample Size

n = (zα/2 σ / E)²
n = p̂(1 − p̂)(zα/2 / E)²
Solve the margin of error E for n. Always round up, and use p̂ = 0.5 when the proportion is unknown.
EXAMPLE
E = 2, σ = 10, 95% → 96.04 → 97
