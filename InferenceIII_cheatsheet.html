Quick Reference
01 · The Claim

Null & Alternative

H₀: = , ≥ , ≤ vs. Hₐ: ≠ , < , >
The null is the status quo (with equality); the alternative is the effect we test for. Its direction sets the tail.

02 · Test Stat, Mean

t Statistic

t = (x̄ − μ₀) / (s/√n)
How many standard errors the sample mean sits from the hypothesized value; df = n − 1.
EXCEL
=(AVERAGE(rng)-μ₀)/(STDEV.S(rng)/SQRT(n))
03 · Test Stat, Proportion

z Statistic

z = (p̂ − p₀) / √(p₀(1−p₀)/n)
The proportion version. The hypothesized p₀ is used in the standard error.
EXCEL
=(p̂-p₀)/SQRT(p₀*(1-p₀)/n)
04 · The Evidence

p-value

P(as extreme | H₀ true)
The chance of data at least this extreme if the null were true. Small p-value = evidence against H₀.
EXCEL
=T.DIST.2T(ABS(t), df)
05 · The Rule

Decision

p-value < α → reject H₀
Reject when the p-value is below the significance level α; otherwise fail to reject.
EXCEL
=T.DIST.RT(t, df) =T.DIST(t, df, TRUE)
06 · Being Wrong

Type I & II Errors

α = false positive, β = false negative
Type I rejects a true null; Type II keeps a false null. Power = 1 − β detects a real effect.
