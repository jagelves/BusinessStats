Quick Reference
01 · Centered

Unbiasedness

E[X̄] = μ
On average, the sample mean equals the population mean — the sampling error cancels out across all samples.

02 · Off the Mark

Sampling Error

X̄ − μ
The gap between a sample statistic and the population parameter. Natural, not a mistake; shrinks as n grows.
EXCEL
—
03 · Spread

Standard Error (Mean)

σx̄ = σ / √n
The standard deviation of the sample mean. Larger samples give a smaller standard error.
EXCEL
=σ / SQRT(n)
04 · Shape

Central Limit Theorem

(X̄ − μ) / (σ/√n) ≈ N(0,1)
For large n, the sample mean is approximately normal — even if the population is not.
EXCEL
=NORM.DIST(x, μ, σ/√n, TRUE)
05 · Proportions

Sample Proportion

p̂ = x/n, se = √(p(1−p)/n)
The sample proportion estimates p and is approximately normal when np ≥ 5 and n(1−p) ≥ 5.
EXCEL
=SQRT(p*(1-p)/n)
06 · Simulate

Random Samples

each column = one sample
Generate many samples at once, then average each column to build a sampling distribution.
EXCEL
=RANDARRAY(rows, cols, min, max, FALSE)
