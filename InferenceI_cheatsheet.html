Sampling Distributions

BUSINESS STATISTICS · J. ALEJANDRO GELVES
Quick Reference
01 · Centered

Unbiasedness

E[X̄] = μ
On average, the sample mean equals the population mean, even though single samples miss it.
EXAMPLE
means 11, 15, 17, 20 → average 15.75 = μ
02 · Off the Mark

Sampling Error

x̄ − μ
The gap between a sample statistic and the parameter. A natural result of sampling, not a mistake. It shrinks as n grows.
EXAMPLE
avg |error|: n = 2 → 4.75, n = 3 → 2.75
03 · Spread

Standard Error (Mean)

σx̄ = σ / √n
The standard deviation of the sample mean. Larger samples give a smaller standard error.
EXCEL
=σ / SQRT(n)
04 · Shape

Central Limit Theorem

(X̄ − μ) / (σ / √n) ≈ N(0, 1)
For large n (n > 30), the sample mean is approximately normal, whatever the shape of the population.
EXCEL
=NORM.DIST(x, μ, σ/SQRT(n), TRUE)
05 · Proportions

Sample Proportion

p̂ = x / n,  se = √(p(1 − p) / n)
Unbiased for p, and approximately normal when np ≥ 5 and n(1 − p) ≥ 5.
EXCEL
=SQRT(p*(1-p)/n)
06 · Simulate

Random Samples

one column = one sample
Fill a block with random numbers, then average each column to build a sampling distribution.
EXCEL
=RANDARRAY(rows, cols, min, max, FALSE)
