Continuous Distributions

BUSINESS STATISTICS · J. ALEJANDRO GELVES
Quick Reference
01 · Constant Density

Uniform

f(x) = 1/(b − a)
Equal density across [a, b]. Cumulative: (x−a)/(b−a).
EXCEL
=(x-a)/(b-a)
02 · Bell Curve

Normal

f(x) = (1/σ√2π) e−½((x−μ)/σ)²
Symmetric, fully described by its mean μ and standard deviation σ.
EXCEL
=NORM.DIST(x, μ, σ, TRUE)
03 · Standardized

Standard Normal

z = (x − μ) / σ
The normal distribution with mean 0 and standard deviation 1.
EXCEL
=NORM.S.DIST(z, TRUE)
04 · Waiting Time

Exponential

f(x) = λe−λx
Time between events in a Poisson process; rate λ = 1/μ.
EXCEL
=EXPON.DIST(x, λ, TRUE)
05 · Three Points

Triangular

F(x) = (x−a)² / (b−a)(c−a)
Used when only the minimum, most-likely, and maximum are known.
EXCEL
=(x-a)^2/((b-a)*(c-a))
06 · Quantiles

Inverse Normal

value ← probability
Go from a probability back to a value, such as a percentile or cutoff.
EXCEL
=NORM.INV(p, μ, σ)
