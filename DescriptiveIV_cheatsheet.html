Measures of Dispersion

BUSINESS STATISTICS · J. ALEJANDRO GELVES
Quick Reference
01 · Simplest

Range

Range = Max − Min
Easy to compute, but it only uses the two most extreme values.
EXCEL
=MAX(range) − MIN(range) · MAXIFS / MINIFS
02 · Squared Deviations

Variance

σ² = Σ(xᵢ − μ)² / N
s² = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n − 1)
Average squared distance from the mean. Squaring removes negative signs, so the units are squared.
EXCEL
=VAR.P(range) · =VAR.S(range)
03 · Original Units

Standard Deviation

σ = √σ²  ·  s = √s²
The square root of the variance. Brings dispersion back to the units of the data.
EXCEL
=STDEV.P(range) · =STDEV.S(range)
04 · Absolute Deviations

Mean Absolute Deviation

mad = Σ|xᵢ − x̄| / n
Average absolute distance from the mean. Less sensitive to large deviations than the standard deviation.
EXCEL
=AVEDEV(range)
05 · Relative Spread

Coefficient of Variation

CV = (s / x̄) × 100
Spread as a percentage of the mean. Use it to compare variables with different units or scales.
EXCEL
=STDEV.S(range) / AVERAGE(range)
06 · Risk Adjusted

Sharpe Ratio

(R̄p − Rf) / s
Excess return over the risk-free rate per unit of risk. A higher ratio is better.
EXCEL
=(AVERAGE(range) − Rf) / STDEV.S(range)
