Quick Reference
01 · Direction

The Covariance

sxy = Σ(xi − x̄)(yi − ȳ) / (n − 1)
Measures the direction of the linear relationship. Its magnitude depends on the units of the data, so it is not comparable across data sets.
EXCEL
=COVARIANCE.S(A1:A10,B1:B10)
02 · Strength & Direction

The Correlation

r = sxy / (sx · sy)
Standardizes the covariance to the range [−1, 1]. It is unit-free and captures both the strength and direction of the linear relationship.
EXCEL
=CORREL(A1:A10,B1:B10)
03 · Explained Variation

Coefficient of Det.

R2 = r2
The percent of the variation in y explained by x. Ranges from 0 (no relationship) to 1 (a perfect linear fit).
EXCEL
=CORREL(A1:A10,B1:B10)^2

05 · Building Block

Standard Deviation

sx = √[ Σ(xi − x̄)² / (n − 1) ]
Correlation divides the covariance by the product of the two standard deviations, sx and sy.
EXCEL
=STDEV.S(A1:A10)
06 · Sample vs Population

Covariance Variants

sample: ÷ (n − 1)
population: ÷ N
Use the .S version when your data is a sample and the .P version when it is the entire population.
EXCEL
=COVARIANCE.P(A1:A10,B1:B10)
