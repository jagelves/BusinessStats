Probability Rules

BUSINESS STATISTICS · J. ALEJANDRO GELVES
Quick Reference
01 · Basics

Probability of an Event

P(A) = favorable / total
The ratio of favorable outcomes to all outcomes. Always between 0 and 1.
EXCEL
=COUNTIFS(rng,crit)/COUNTA(rng)
02 · Not A

Complement Rule

P(Ac) = 1 − P(A)
The chance an event does not occur.
EXCEL
=1 - A2
03 · A or B

Addition Rule

P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)
The chance A or B (or both) occur; subtract the overlap so it is not double-counted.
EXCEL
=A2 + B2 - C2
04 · A and B

Multiplication Rule

P(A∩B) = P(A|B)·P(B)
The chance both occur. If independent, P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B).
EXCEL
=A2 * B2
05 · Updating

Bayes' Theorem

P(A|B) = P(B|A)·P(A) / P(B)
Revises a probability after new evidence is observed.
EXCEL
=(B2*A2) / C2
06 · Counting

Factorial · Combos · Perms

n! , C(n,x) , P(n,x)
Count arrangements without listing them. Combinations ignore order; permutations respect it.
EXCEL
=FACT(n) =COMBIN(n,x) =PERMUT(n,x)
