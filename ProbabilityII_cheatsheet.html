Quick Reference
01 · Center

Expected Value

E(x) = Σ x·f(x)
The long-run average of a random variable.
EXCEL
=SUMPRODUCT(x_range, p_range)
02 · Spread

Variance

var(x) = E(x2) − [E(x)]2
The average squared deviation from the mean.
EXCEL
=SUMPRODUCT((x-μ)^2, p_range)
03 · Equally Likely

Discrete Uniform

f(x) = 1/n
Every one of the n outcomes is equally likely.
EXCEL
=1/n
04 · Successes in n Trials

Binomial

f(x) = Cxn px(1−p)n−x
Successes in n independent trials. E(x)=np, var=np(1−p).
EXCEL
=BINOM.DIST(x, n, p, FALSE)
05 · Without Replacement

Hypergeometric

f(x) = Cxr Cn−xN−r / CnN
Successes drawn from a finite population without replacement.
EXCEL
=HYPGEOM.DIST(x, n, r, N, FALSE)
06 · Rare Events

Poisson

f(x) = μx e−μ / x!
Counts over an interval of time or space. Mean = variance = μ.
EXCEL
=POISSON.DIST(x, μ, FALSE)
