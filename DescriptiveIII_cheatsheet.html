Measures of Central Location

BUSINESS STATISTICS · J. ALEJANDRO GELVES
Quick Reference
01 · Most Common

Arithmetic Mean

x̄ = Σxᵢ / n  ·  μ = Σxᵢ / N
Adds every value and divides by the count. Because it uses every value, outliers pull it toward them.
EXCEL
=AVERAGE(range)
02 · Middle Value

Median

middle of the sorted data
Sort the data and take the middle value. If n is even, average the two middle values. Robust to outliers.
EXCEL
=MEDIAN(range)
03 · Most Frequent

Mode

value with the highest frequency
Works for categorical data. A variable may have no mode, one mode, or several (bimodal).
EXCEL
=MODE(range) · returns one mode
04 · Importance Weights

Weighted Mean

x̄w = Σwᵢxᵢ / Σwᵢ
Gives some observations more importance than others, as in portfolios, GPAs, or surveys.
EXCEL
=SUMPRODUCT(w, x) / SUM(w)
05 · Rates

Geometric Mean

x̄g = ⁿ√[(1+r₁)⋯(1+rₙ)] − 1
For returns or growth rates that compound. Add 1 to each rate, take the nth root of the product, subtract 1.
EXCEL
=GEOMEAN(1+r₁, …, 1+rₙ) − 1
06 · Levels

Geometric Mean

x̄g = ⁿ√(x₁ × ⋯ × xₙ)
For positive levels such as prices, proportions, or index values. No adjustment needed.
EXCEL
=GEOMEAN(range)
